The studio

Masala Studios combines technical precision with expressive storytelling to create audiovisual work that travels across formats, audiences, and platforms. The studio's identity is built around impact, emotion, and versatility rather than fixed creative formulas.

Led by Javier Ponce Berrio, the studio brings decades of production experience across film, television, music, live events, and branded projects.
